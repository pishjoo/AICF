# AICF - AI Content Factory

## Vision

AICF is an autonomous AI content production factory.

The system helps creators produce YouTube videos from idea discovery to final video.

---

# Main Workflow

1. Trend Discovery
2. Topic Selection
3. Research
4. Story Development
5. Script Writing
6. User Approval
7. Scene Planning
8. Image Generation
9. Voice Generation
10. Video Editing
11. Publishing

---

# Content Profile

Each channel has its own profile.

Example:

Historical Channel:

- Old cinematic visual style
- Ancient atmosphere
- Documentary storytelling
- Specific hashtags
- Preferred sources
- Forbidden topics

Cooking Channel:

- Vertical videos
- 30 seconds duration
- Specific music style
- Food photography rules
